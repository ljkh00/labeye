Building a
Cybersecurity Training Lab
from Scratch

A phased, student-driven transformation of a physical computer lab into a fully virtualised, centrally managed Security Operations Centre training environment — built on existing hardware with zero commercial licensing cost where possible.

8 FYP candidates · 2 semesters · 3 phases
Each candidate owns one functional layer. Together they form one coherent system.

⚠ PROBLEM STATEMENT

The lab currently runs 10 standalone Acer Predator PCs connected through OPNSense with no centralised management, no monitoring, and no repeatable lab deployment. Each session requires manual setup. Security exercises (Red/Blue Team) cannot be conducted safely or at scale. New Lenovo PCs arriving as tech refresh create an opportunity to restructure entirely.

✓ PROPOSED SOLUTION

Virtualise the 10 Acer PCs as edge hypervisors (Proxmox) managed by a Dell R440 master node. Deploy 6 student FYP projects across 2 semesters to build — layer by layer — a complete virtualised SOC training platform covering image management, network segmentation, automation, monitoring, attack/defence labs, IDS/IPS, honeypots and a self-service orchestration portal.
